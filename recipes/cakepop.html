Kingdom Hearts Paopu fruit Cake Pops

It is said that if two people share one their destinies become intertwined forever!

Description

These are perfect for new years parties or for when you're looking for something special to share with someone special. Aside from being delicious super delicious they might just tie you and someone else's destinies together if you share one.

Ingredients

ingredients for cake

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 3/4 tsp baking soda
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp Vanilla extract
  • 6 egg whites
  • 2 cups champagne or sparkling cider
  • 3 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup butter
  • candy melts: yellow and green

Ingredients for frosting

  • 3 cups powdered sugar
  • 3 sticks unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • pinch of salt
  • 1/4 cup champagne or apple cider

Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 350F. Line and grease a 9'x13' baking pan
  2. In a medium sized bowl, beat together the butter and sugar until light and fluffy
  3. Add the egg whites and mix until well combined
  4. Add the vanilla extract then set aside
  5. In another medium bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, baking powser and baking soda.
  6. Alternate adding the dry ingredients and champagne to the butter mixture, starting and ending with the dry ingredients until well combined
  7. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 30 minutes, let cool completely
  8. Make the frosting in a large stand mixer bowl, beat the butter on medium speed for 8 minutes
  9. Add the powdered sugar, vanilla extract, pinch of salt, champagne or apple cider and beat for another 3 minutes on low speed
  10. Crumble the cake in a large bowl and mix with frosting until well combined

Time to Decorate

  1. Melt the yellow candy melts and transfer to a decorating bag
  2. Pipe a thin layer of the candy melts on the star mold, Use a toothpick to cover the sides
  3. Place it in the freezer for about 10 minutes to chill
  4. Take about a teaspoon of the cake pop mix and press gently into the star mold.
  5. Place a lollipop stick on top
  6. Pipe more candy melts and cover the back completely
  7. Place it in the freezer for another 10 minutes to set
  8. Melt the green candy melts and transfer to a decorating bag with a #2 tip
  9. Pipe the leaf on top of the star.
  10. Enjoy!
