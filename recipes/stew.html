Dwarven Stew

This delicious Dwarven Stew is inspired by Divinity Original Sin 2

Description

In a world full of advenutre even adventurers deserve a delicious meal from time to time, and so do you, this stew might be simple but it sure is filling and delicious especially after a long day of adventuring or work, or when you're looking for something cozy and delcious during those cold winter night's. whatever the situation this stew's got you covered

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs beef chuck, cut into 1 inch cubes
  • 1/2 cup (80g) flour
  • 2 tsp (8g) salt
  • 1 1/2 (2g) pepper
  • 2 tsp (3g) dried thyme
  • 1 tsp (1g) ground sage
  • 1/2 tsp (1g) ground allspice
  • 1/4 tsp (0.5g) ground cloves
  • Canola oil
  • 4 onions, thinly sliced
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 carrots, cut into bite sized pieces
  • 1/3 cup (68 g) brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp (30 ml) cornstarch
  • 1 cup (250 ml) beef broth
  • 1 1/2 cups (375 ml) of trappist ale
  • 2 bay leaves

Preperation

  1. preheat oven to 300'f. in a large ziplock bag, combine flour, salt, pepper, dried thyme, ground sage, ground allspice, and ground cloves, place several pieces of beef, shake to coat, and transfer to a plate, this may be done in batches,
  2. Heat a dutch oven over medium heat and add 1 tbsp of canola oil, Add a single layer of beef but becareful not to overcrowed the pan, Brown all sides of the meat. Remove and place on a plate. Add additional canola oil and continue this process until all the beef has been browned.
  3. Add the onions and, if needed, additional canola oil. Saute the onions until caramelized (about 15 minutes)
  4. Add the brown s ugar and garlic, Cook for 2 minutes
  5. Add the beef, an juices, and carrots on the plate and stir everything together.
  6. Combine the beef stock and cornstarch together in a bowl and whisk until smooth, Add to the dutch oven and stir together.
  7. Add the trappist ale to the dutch oven and stir together. Finally, Add the bay leaves, Cover and place in the oven and cook for 2-3 hours, until the meat is tender. I do recommend checking and lightly stirring the stew every half hour while it cooks, During the last half hour of cooking, uncover.
  8. When finished serve with buttered noodles and your choice of bread

I hope you enjoyed making this recipe and get to enjoy during those fall/witer nights, Enjoy!
